### Three corrections before implementation

**1. Test C has a calculation inconsistency.**
At 40 kW demand:

```text
Generator = 15 kW
Remaining = 25 kW
Battery max = 20 kW
```

So the system **cannot supply the full 40 kW at any point**. ESH should represent the time until the battery can no longer cover the 25 kW deficit, but the test must explicitly account for the 20 kW battery power limit rather than saying “battery drains at 25 kW.”

**2. The instantaneous formula needs careful semantics.**
If demand exceeds `generator + battery_max_discharge`, the system cannot meet the load continuously. Returning a battery-energy-derived duration is acceptable only as a **time-to-power-failure estimate**, and should be documented as such.

**3. Avoid duplicated physical constants if possible.**
The config approach is good for Phase A, but long-term these values should come from the actual Battery/Generator configuration rather than being maintained in two places.
